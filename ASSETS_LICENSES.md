# Assets & Licenses (ASSETS_LICENSES.md)

## 1. Zero-Copyright Legal Compliance
K-Drama Crosswords is an independent, 100% offline premium puzzle title. In strict accordance with commercial IP safety guidelines:
- **No Copyrighted Imagery**: No broadcast stills, posters, studio logos, promotional photos, or actor likenesses are distributed or fetched.
- **Original Vector Art & Open-Source Assets**: All game icons, badges, UI elements, and brand motifs (e.g. Soju bottle & finger heart) are original geometric flat designs or open-source vector glyphs.
- **Fair-Use Fan Trivia**: All trivia, clues, and behind-the-scenes facts are factual descriptions of artistic works, strictly verified against at least two public independent sources (e.g., Nielsen Korea viewership reports, Baeksang Arts Awards records, TMDb metadata, Wikipedia, official network press releases).

1. **Nielsen Korea (AGB Nielson)** / TNmS: Official nationwide television viewership ratings.

5. **Yonhap News Agency / The Korea Herald / Variety / BBC**: Production press conferences, broadcast scheduling announcements, and international acclaim.
