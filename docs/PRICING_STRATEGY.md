# Pricing & Commercialization Strategy: K-Drama Crosswords

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**K-Drama Crosswords** is packaged as a **Premium, Paid Upfront Application** on Google Play. 

- **Target Launch Price**: **₩5,000 KRW** / **$4.99 USD** (Tier 5 on Google Play Pricing Matrix)
- **Revenue Model**: 100% upfront premium purchase.
- **Monetization Inclusions**:
  - Zero advertisements (no banners, interstitials, or rewarded video ads).
  - Zero In-App Purchases (IAP).
  - Zero subscriptions or paywalled puzzle packs.
  - Full access to all 400 launch puzzle levels and 163 collectible trophies upon initial download.

---

## 2. Rationale for Paid Upfront vs. Free-to-Play (F2P)

| Attribute | Free-to-Play Ad/IAP Model | Premium Paid Model (Our Choice) |
|---|---|---|
| **User Experience** | Interrupted by video ads every 2–3 levels; friction-heavy energy timers. | Seamless, serene puzzle solving with uninterrupted focus and instant 60 FPS response. |
| **Data Privacy & Legal Risk** | Ad networks (AdMob, Unity) collect device IDs, IP addresses, and behavioral data, triggering complex COPPA/GDPR liabilities. | **Zero Data / Zero Tracking**. No network permissions, no user profiling, zero legal exposure. |
| **Offline Playability** | Often fails without an active internet connection to load ad waterfalls. | **100% Offline**. Perfect for subways, commutes, flights, and low-connectivity regions. |
| **Content Perception** | Viewed as disposable or ad-bait clone software. | Perceived as a handcrafted, high-quality collector's tribute to South Korean drama culture. |
| **LTV / Conversion** | Typical casual puzzle eCPM yields < $0.03 per session. | Guaranteed $3.50+ net proceeds per install immediately after Google platform fee (15% for Google Play Play Media Experience / auto-reduced tier). |

## 4. In-Game Economy Balance

Although the app is paid upfront, it features an engaging gamified economy to provide rewarding progression:
- **Starting Bank**: 100 virtual coins upon first install.
- **Solve Rewards**:
  - +50 Coins per solved word in Classic Crosswords.
  - +100 Coins per solved riddle in Emoji Rebus.
  - +200 Coins level completion bonus.
- **Hint Shop Costs**:
  - 50 Coins: Reveal First Letter.
  - 100 Coins: Reveal Random Letter.
  - 250 Coins: Reveal Entire Word / Title.
- **Self-Sustaining Loop**: Players earn between 450 to 800 coins per completed puzzle, ensuring that hints are readily accessible through skilled play without requiring artificial microtransactions.
