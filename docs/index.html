Privacy Policy for K-Drama Crosswords

Application: K-Drama Crosswords (com.kdramacrosswords.app)
Last Updated: October 29, 2026 | Effective Date: October 29, 2026

1. Summary: Zero Data Collection Principle

K-Drama Crosswords ("we," "our," or "the App") does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or device data of any kind.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, create a profile, or provide an email address.
  • No Network Transmission: The App operates 100% offline. The Android application manifest does not request android.permission.INTERNET.
  • No Third-Party SDKs: The App contains zero advertising networks, analytics frameworks, tracking pixels, or diagnostic SDKs.
  • No Device Tracking: We do not access Android Advertising ID (AAID), hardware MAC addresses, IMEI, IP addresses, or device fingerprints.

2. Local Storage and Data Persistence

All game data generated during gameplay is stored exclusively in the App's private local sandboxed directory on your physical device:

  • Game Progress: Levels completed, star ratings, and sequential level unlock statuses.
  • In-Game Currency: Virtual coins earned through gameplay solves and spent in the hint shop.
  • Unlocked Trophies: Collectible trivia lore cards unlocked upon level completion.
  • Preferences: Display settings such as Dark Mode.
  • Daily Challenge Streak: Consecutive daily puzzle clear records.

This data never leaves your device and is completely inaccessible to external servers or third parties.

3. Data Deletion and Retention

Because all game data resides strictly on your local device:

  • Uninstalling the App: Removing the application from your device permanently deletes all locally stored save files, progress, coins, and preferences.
  • Clearing App Data: You can reset all game progress at any time via Android Settings > Apps > K-Drama Crosswords > Storage > Clear Data.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

The App is designed for general audiences and is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K):

  • We do not collect personal information from children or adults.
  • The App contains no chat features, social links, web redirects, or targeted advertisements.

5. Permissions Requested

The App requests zero dangerous or runtime permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: NOT REQUESTED
  • android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION: NOT REQUESTED
  • android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: NOT REQUESTED
  • android.permission.READ_CONTACTS: NOT REQUESTED
  • android.permission.CAMERA: NOT REQUESTED
