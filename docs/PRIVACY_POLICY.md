# Privacy Policy for K-Drama Crosswords

**Last Updated:** October 29, 2026  
**Effective Date:** October 29, 2026

**K-Drama Crosswords** ("we," "our," or "the App"), package identifier `com.kdramacrosswords.app`, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and disclosure.

---

## 1. Summary: Zero Data Collection Principle

**K-Drama Crosswords does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or device data of any kind.**

- **No Account Required**: You do not need to register, create a profile, or provide an email address.
- **No Network Requests**: The App operates 100% offline. The Android application manifest does not request `android.permission.INTERNET` or `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`.
- **No Third-Party SDKs**: The App contains no advertising networks, analytics frameworks, tracking pixels, or diagnostic SDKs.
- **No Tracking or Identifiers**: We do not access Android Advertising ID (AAID), hardware MAC addresses, IMEI, IP addresses, or device fingerprints.

---

## 2. Local Storage and Data Persistence

All game data generated during your gameplay is stored **exclusively in the App's private local sandboxed directory on your physical device**:
- **Game Progress**: Levels completed, star ratings, and sequential level unlock statuses.
- **In-Game Currency**: Virtual coins earned through gameplay solves and spent on hints.
- **Unlocked Trophies**: Collectible trivia lore cards unlocked upon level completion.
- **Preferences**: Display settings such as Dark Mode and sound effect toggles.
- **Daily Challenge Streak**: Timestamp and counter for consecutive daily puzzle clears.

This data never leaves your device and is inaccessible to other applications or third parties.

---

## 3. Data Deletion and Retention

Because all game data resides strictly on your local device:
- **Uninstalling the App**: Removing the application from your device immediately and permanently deletes all locally stored save files, progress, coins, and preferences.
- **Clearing App Data**: You can reset all game progress at any time by navigating to **Android Settings > Apps > K-Drama Crosswords > Storage > Clear Data**.

We do not operate remote servers, so we have no copies of your gameplay data.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

The App is designed for general audiences and is fully compliant with the **Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K)**:
- We do not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).
- The App contains no chat features, social links, external web redirects, or targeted advertisements.

---

## 5. Permissions Requested

The App requests **zero dangerous or runtime permissions**. Specifically:
- `android.permission.INTERNET`: **NOT REQUESTED**
- `android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`: **NOT REQUESTED**
- `android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`: **NOT REQUESTED**
- `android.permission.READ_CONTACTS`: **NOT REQUESTED**
- `android.permission.CAMERA`: **NOT REQUESTED**

---

## 6. Third-Party Links & Content

The App contains no external web links or embedded web views during gameplay. Dual-source citations referenced in the Trophy Hall are recorded as informational text references and are not hyperlinked to external websites.
